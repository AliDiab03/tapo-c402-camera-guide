Privacy Policy

App: Tapo C402 Camera Guide (com.mohadev.tapoc402guide)
Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tapo C402 Camera Guide Android app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is a simple guide: it has no accounts, no sign-up, and we do not collect your personal information ourselves.

Unofficial app notice: The App is an independent, unofficial guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TP-Link or Tapo. Product names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to describe the product the guide covers. The App does not connect to, control, or access your camera or your Tapo account.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information on our own servers. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone, and it does not use any video or images from your security camera.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a few simple settings only on your device, such as which setup and placement steps you ticked, which guide sections you visited, and your badge progress. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

3. Permissions

The App uses only these permissions:

  • INTERNET: to display ads and to open web pages you choose to visit.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to check whether your device is online.
  • AD_ID (Advertising ID): to allow Google's advertising services to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain data to show and measure ads, such as:

  • Advertising ID and similar device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system, language)
  • App interaction and ad performance data

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not by us. You can learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use partner sites or apps

5. Consent and your choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and some other regions), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time using the Ad privacy choices button in the App, when it is available for your region.

You can also:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Opt out of personalized ads in your device's ad settings.

6. Web pages and external links

The App can open web pages inside the App, such as store search pages (for example Amazon, Walmart, and Best Buy) and the TP-Link website. Those websites are operated by third parties and have their own privacy practices and may use cookies or similar technologies. We are not responsible for their content or policies. Any purchase you make is a transaction between you and that store.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not store any on our servers. Data handled by third-party services such as Google AdMob is protected and retained according to their own policies.

9. Security camera and privacy reminder

The App provides general information only. If you install a security camera, please follow your local laws and respect the privacy of neighbors and visitors. Do not point cameras at private areas. Camera settings, recordings, and your Tapo account are managed in the official Tapo app and are governed by the manufacturer's own privacy policy.
